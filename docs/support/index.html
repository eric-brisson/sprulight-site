SpruLight support

SpruLight tracks your tabletop miniatures collection and the time you spend assembling and painting each model, on iPad and iPhone.

Contact

Email info@sprulight.com. Include your app version (Settings → About) and what you were doing when the problem happened.

Questions

What do I need to run SpruLight?

An iPad or an iPhone with iOS or iPadOS 26 or later. On iPhone the app is used in portrait.

Where is my data?

Only on your device. SpruLight has no account and no server, so each device keeps its own data and nothing is synced between them. See the privacy policy.

How do I keep my data safe?

Keep your device's iCloud or computer backups turned on; they include the app's data and your journal photos. Deleting the app deletes its data.

How do I free up space used by photos?

Settings → Storage shows how much space your journal photos take. Photos are stored only on your device. To free space, delete journal entries from a model's history or a unit's journal.

The app says "Can't open your data."

Your data hasn't been changed or deleted. Update to the latest version of the app. If the message stays, email the error details shown on that screen to info@sprulight.com.
