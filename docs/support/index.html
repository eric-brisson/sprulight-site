SpruLight support

SpruLight tracks your tabletop miniatures collection and the time you spend assembling and painting each model, on iPad and iPhone.

Contact

Email info@sprulight.com. Include your app version (Settings → About) and what you were doing when the problem happened.

Questions

What do I need to run SpruLight?

An iPad or an iPhone with iOS or iPadOS 26 or later. On iPhone the app is used in portrait.

Where is my data?

On your device, and in your own iCloud storage when iCloud is on for SpruLight. SpruLight has no account of its own and no server; the developer can't see your data. See the privacy policy.

How do I sync between my iPad and iPhone?

Sign in to iCloud with the same Apple ID on both, and make sure iCloud is on for SpruLight (Settings app → your name → iCloud). Your collection, recorded time, journal (photos included) and paints then sync by themselves. In SpruLight, Settings → iCloud shows whether syncing is on.

If a device that already has its own data finds data in iCloud the first time, SpruLight asks which to keep: Use iCloud's Data (this device's data is moved to a backup, not deleted), Merge (keeps both), or Not Now.

Something I saved on my other device doesn't show.

Changes usually arrive within seconds while both apps are open, and every time the app starts. If one is missing, choose Sync Now in the … menu. The app reloads its data from iCloud and opens on its first screen.

How do I keep my data safe?

Keep iCloud on for SpruLight, or keep your device's iCloud or computer backups turned on; they include the app's data and your journal photos. Deleting the app deletes the data on that device. What's in iCloud stays there and comes back when you reinstall.

How do I delete my data from iCloud?

Open the Settings app → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → SpruLight → Delete Data. This removes it from iCloud and from every device that syncs it.

How do I free up space used by photos?

Settings → Storage shows how much space your journal photos take. With iCloud on, photos also count against your iCloud storage. To free space, delete journal entries from a model's history or a unit's journal.

The app says "Can't open your data."

Your data hasn't been changed or deleted. Update to the latest version of the app. If the message stays, email the error details shown on that screen to info@sprulight.com.
