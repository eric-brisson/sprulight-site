Features

Everything SpruLight can do.

From your collection to the final coat of paint, SpruLight keeps your work, history and hobby details together.

Collection Atelier Tracking Journal Paints Progress iPhone

Collection

Start with what you own.

  • Built-in catalog

    Add the box sets you own from the catalog for StarCraft: The Miniatures Game.

  • Models added automatically

    Each box brings its units and individual models, numbered unit by unit.

  • Boxes or factions

    Browse your collection box by box, or see it by faction.

  • Choose what you're working on

    Start building a box and it appears in the Atelier. The rest stay out of the way.

Atelier

Your active workbench.

  • Only what's active

    The Atelier shows only the boxes you're working on.

  • One row per unit. One tile per model.

    Tap a tile to open the model and start work.

  • Rings that show progress

    One half is assembly, the other painting. An outline is in progress, solid is done.

  • Running work stays visible

    A running timer marks its tile, and a Session row at the top takes you back to it.

Tracking

Track the work, not just the result.

Assembly and painting are timed separately.

Assembly

  • Timed per model

    Start assembly from a model and a clock runs. Every model keeps its own time.

  • Pause, resume or stop

    Pause when you step away. Stop saves the time without completing the stage.

  • Finish when it's built

    Finish saves the time and marks the model assembled.

Individual painting

  • One model at a time

    Start a painting session on a model, pause and resume, and finish when it's painted.

  • Its own time

    Painting time is tracked separately from assembly.

  • Add time by hand

    Forgot to start the timer? Add the time yourself.

Batch painting

  • Pick what you're painting

    In Batch Paint, select assembled models ready for painting, or a whole unit at once.

  • One shared session

    Start one painting session for all of them. The time is divided equally.

  • Write once, add to all

    One journal entry and one paint selection apply to every model in the batch.

Journal

Keep the story, not just the status.

  • Notes and photos

    Add notes, photos or both to any model. Take a photo or choose one from your library.

  • Tagged by stage

    Every entry belongs to Assembly or Painting, so the history keeps its context.

  • A history for every model

    Open a model to see its entries, paints and recorded sessions, or browse the Journal across everything by day, stage, faction, box, unit or photos.

Paints

Remember what you used.And find what is close.

  • Paints on every model

    Record the paints you used on a model, or on a whole batch.

  • See where you used it

    Open a paint to see every model it went on.

  • Mark what you own

    Keep track of your paints and look at just those.

  • 5,000+ paints

    Browse paints from different manufacturers.

  • Similar colors across brands

    Find paints with similar colors in other brands. Matches are close, not necessarily exact.

  • Add your own

    Add a custom paint when the library is missing one.

Progress

See the hobby add up.

  • By box

    Models assembled and painted in a box, and the time that went into it.

  • By faction

    The same progress across everything you own.

  • Filter by stage

    Narrow everything to one stage to compare.

  • Time totals

    Totals for boxes, units and models, with assembly and painting counted separately.

On iPhone

Useful even when the app is closed.

  • Lock Screen

    The running timer sits on your Lock Screen with its clock.

  • Dynamic Island

    It appears there too, on iPhones that have one.

  • Pause, resume, stop

    Control the session without opening the app.

  • Keeps running

    The session carries on while the app is closed.

Privacy

Yours stays yours.

  • Stored on your device

    Your collection, journal and photos stay on your device.

  • No account. No ads. No tracking.

    No account to create, no ads and no usage tracking.

  • Works without internet

    SpruLight works entirely offline and doesn't transmit your collection, journal, photos or painting activity.
