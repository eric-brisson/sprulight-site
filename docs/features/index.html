Features

Everything SpruLight helps you keep track of.

SpruLight follows your miniatures from collection through assembly, painting and the finished shelf.

Collection Atelier Tracking Journal Paints Progress iPhone

Collection

Start with what you own.

  • Built-in catalog

    Add the box sets you own from the catalog for StarCraft: The Miniatures Game. No typing in models by hand.

  • Models created for you

    SpruLight creates the units and the individual models in each box, numbered unit by unit.

  • Boxes or factions

    Browse your collection box by box, or switch to factions and see it from that side.

  • Choose what you're working on

    Start building a box and it appears in the Atelier. The boxes you aren't working on stay out of the way.

Atelier

Your active workbench.

  • Only what's active

    The Atelier shows the boxes you're working on, and nothing else.

  • A row per unit, a tile per model

    Tap a tile to open the model and start work from there.

  • Rings that show progress

    One half of the ring is assembly, the other half is painting. An outline means in progress, solid means done.

  • Running work stays visible

    While a timer runs, its tile is marked and a Session row at the top takes you straight back to it.

Tracking

Track the work, not just the result.

Assembly

  • Timed per model

    Start assembly from a model and a clock runs while you work. Every model keeps its own time.

  • Pause, resume or stop

    Pause when you step away. Stop saves the time without completing the stage.

  • Finish when it's built

    Finish saves the time and marks the model assembled.

Individual painting

  • One model at a time

    Start a painting session on a model, pause and resume as you go, and finish when it's painted.

  • Its own time

    Painting time is tracked separately from assembly, for every model.

  • Add time by hand

    Forgot to start the timer? Add the time yourself.

Batch painting

  • Pick the models that are ready

    In Batch Paint, select the assembled models you're about to paint, or a whole unit at once.

  • One shared session

    Start a single painting session for all of them. The time is divided equally between the models.

  • Write once, add to all

    One journal entry and one paint selection apply to every model in the batch.

Journal

Keep the story, not just the status.

  • Notes and photos

    Add a note, photos or both to any model. Take a photo or choose one from your library.

  • Tagged by stage

    Every entry belongs to the stage you were on, assembly or painting.

  • A history per model

    Open a model to read its entries, the paints used and every recorded session in one place.

  • A journal across everything

    The Journal tab lists entries newest first, by day. Search it, filter by stage, faction, box or unit, or show only entries with photos.

Paints

Remember what you used.And find what is close.

  • Paints on every model

    Record the paints you used on a model, or on a whole batch at once.

  • Where it was used

    Open a paint to see every model it went on.

  • Mark what you own

    Keep track of the paints you have, and look at just those.

  • Over 5,000 paints

    Browse a library of paints from different manufacturers.

  • Similar colors across brands

    Find paints with similar colors in other brands. Matches are close, not necessarily exact.

  • Add your own

    Add a custom paint when the library is missing one.

Progress

See the hobby add up.

  • By box

    See how many models in a box are assembled and painted, and how much time went into it.

  • By faction

    Switch to factions to see the same progress across everything you own.

  • Assembly or painting

    Narrow everything to one stage when you want to compare.

  • Time totals

    Time is totalled for boxes, units and models, with assembly and painting counted separately.

On iPhone

Useful even when the app is closed.

  • On the Lock Screen

    A running timer appears on your Lock Screen.

  • In the Dynamic Island

    And in the Dynamic Island, on iPhones that have one.

  • Pause and Stop within reach

    Pause, resume or stop the session without opening the app.

  • Keeps running

    The session carries on while the app is closed.

Privacy

Yours stays yours.

  • Stored on your device

    Your collection, journal and photos stay on your device.

  • No account, no ads, no tracking

    SpruLight has none of the three.

  • Works without internet

    SpruLight works entirely offline and doesn't transmit your collection, journal, photos or painting activity.
