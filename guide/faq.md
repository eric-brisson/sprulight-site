# FAQ

## Does SpruLight need an account?

No. SpruLight has no account of its own and no server. If iCloud is on, your data syncs through your own iCloud account, which the developer can't read.

## Does it sync between my iPad and iPhone?

Yes, through iCloud. Sign in with the same Apple ID on both, and make sure iCloud is on for SpruLight (Settings app → your name → iCloud). Your collection, recorded time, journal (photos included) and paints then sync by themselves. The paint catalog comes with the app and stays on each device.

If something you saved on one device doesn't show on the other, choose **Sync Now** in the **…** menu.

## Can I keep everything on one device?

Yes. Turn off iCloud for SpruLight in the Settings app (your name → iCloud). SpruLight keeps working with the data on your device.

## Does it work offline?

Yes. SpruLight works without an internet connection. When iCloud is on, it sends your data only to your own iCloud account, never to the developer.

## How do I delete my data from iCloud?

Deleting the app doesn't remove what's in iCloud: it comes back when you reinstall SpruLight. To delete it, open the Settings app → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → SpruLight → Delete Data. This removes it from iCloud and from every device that syncs it.

## What do I need to run it?

An iPad or iPhone with iOS 26 or later.
