# Notes, photos and stages

Keep the story, not just the status.

## Add an entry

Add a note, a photo, or both to any model. Take a photo or choose one from your library.

## Stage tags

Every entry belongs to **Assembly** or **Painting**, so the history keeps its context.

## Browse the history

Open a model to see its entries, paints and recorded sessions. The Journal tab shows everything by day, stage, faction, box, unit or photos.
