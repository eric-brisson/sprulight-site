# What is SpruLight?

SpruLight tracks your miniatures collection and the time you spend assembling and painting each model. It runs on iPad and iPhone.

## What it keeps together

- **Collection**: the box sets you own, with their units and models.
- **Atelier**: the boxes you are working on right now.
- **Sessions**: timed assembly and painting, per model.
- **Journal**: notes and photos, tagged by stage.
- **Paints**: what you used, and where.

## Private by design

Your collection, journal and photos stay on your device. There is no account, no ads and no usage tracking, and SpruLight works fully offline.
