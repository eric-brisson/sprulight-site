# Add your first box

Start with what you own.

## Add a box from the catalog

1. Open **Collection**.
2. Choose to add a box set and pick it from the built-in catalog.
3. The box appears in your collection with its units and individual models, numbered unit by unit.

## Start building

Starting a box moves it into the [Atelier](/atelier/tiles-and-rings). The rest of your collection stays out of the way until you need it.
