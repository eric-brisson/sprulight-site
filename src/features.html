Features

Everything around the miniature, in one place.

SpruLight follows your models from the moment they enter your collection through assembly, painting and the finished shelf.

Collection Atelier Assembly Painting Journal Paints Progress

Collection

Start with what you own.

Browse the built-in catalog for StarCraft: The Miniatures Game and add the box you own. SpruLight creates the models in it, numbered unit by unit.

When you're ready to start, choose Start building. The box appears in your Atelier, which shows only the boxes you're working on.

Atelier

Your workbench, without the clutter.

The Atelier shows the boxes you're working on, one row of tiles per unit. Each tile is a model, and its ring says where it stands: one half for assembly, one for painting. An outline means in progress, solid means done.

Tap a tile to open the model, and start work from there.

Assembly

Build it. Time it. Move on.

Open a model and start assembly. A clock runs on the model's screen while you work.

Pause when life gets in the way, stop to record the time, finish when it's built. Every model keeps its own time, so you can see what a single model really took.

Painting

Painting works the way hobby sessions actually do.

Paint one model on its own, or paint several at once. In Batch Paint, pick the models that are ready, start one session and get painting.

The time is shared equally between them. Write one journal entry or pick the paints once, and it's added to every model in the batch.

Journal

Keep the story, not just the status.

Add notes and photos to any model, tagged with the stage you were on. Over time the journal becomes the story of how each miniature came together.

Look back by day in the Journal tab, or open a model to see its own history.

Paints

Remember what you used.And find what's close.

Add the paints you used to each model, and every paint remembers which models it went on.

Browse a library of over 5,000 paints across different manufacturers, mark the ones you own, and discover paints with similar colors across brands.

Progress

See the hobby add up.

See how far a box has come, or how a whole faction is doing: how many models are assembled and painted, and how much time went into each.

Narrow it to assembly or painting when you want to look at one stage.

On iPhone

Useful even when the app is closed.

A running timer appears on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, with Pause and Stop within reach. Close the app, put the phone down and carry on painting.

Yours stays yours.

Your collection, journal and photos stay on your device. There's no account, no ads and no tracking, and SpruLight works without an internet connection.
