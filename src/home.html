Atelier

Every miniature has a journey.

SpruLight follows each model through assembly and painting. The ring on every tile fills in as you work: one half for assembly, one for painting.

Start a timer on one model, or paint several at once and time them as a batch. It keeps running on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.

Journal

Remember the process.

Add notes and photos to any model, then look back at how it came together: what you wrote, the paints you used and the time each stage took.

Paints

Find similar paints across brands.

Discover paints with similar colors from a library of over 5,000 paints across different manufacturers.

Mark the paints you own and see which models each one was used on.

Progress

See your hobby add up.

Follow your progress by box set or by faction: how many models are assembled and painted, and how much time went into each.

Collection

Everything you own, in one place.

Add the box sets you own from the built-in catalog for StarCraft: The Miniatures Game, and SpruLight sets up each box's models for you.

Pick the boxes you're working on, and they appear in your Atelier.
