Miniature painting tracker

Know where every model stands.

SpruLight tracks your miniatures collection and the time you spend assembling and painting each model.

Coming soon to the App Store

For iPad and iPhone with iOS 26 or later.

Time every model

Start a timer on a model while you assemble or paint it, and pause when life gets in the way. Painting several models at once? Time them as a batch and the time is shared between them. The clock keeps running on your Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, even if you close the app.

See progress at a glance

Every model is a tile with a ring: one half for assembly, one half for painting. It fills in as you work. Follow your progress by box set or by faction.

Keep a build journal

Write notes and add photos for any model, tagged by stage, and record the paints you used on it.

Your collection, box by box

Add the box sets you own from the built-in catalog for StarCraft: The Miniatures Game, and SpruLight sets up each box's models for you. A wishlist keeps track of the ones you're still after.

Find similar paints across brands

Discover paints with similar colors from a library of over 5,000 paints across different manufacturers.

Private by design

Your collection and your photos stay on your device. There's no account to create, no ads, no tracking, and no internet connection needed.
